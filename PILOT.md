# Client pilot plan — proposed, not performed

Five discovery questions:
1. Which omissions caused the most rework in your last twenty completed jobs?
2. Who has authority to approve each type of change, and how is that authority evidenced?
3. Which source formats, quote styles and attachment revisions appear most often?
4. Which fields and combinations must block release for labels, sleeves and cartons?
5. Who would buy this, what system would it replace, and what budget or procurement constraints apply?

Baseline: select twenty completed jobs and their actual final signed specifications with customer consent. Record coordinator minutes from opening the thread to a reconciled order, number of clarifications, production-critical omissions, and incorrectly preferred revisions. A second reviewer adjudicates disagreements. Exclude waiting for customer replies from active review time and report it separately.

Shadow trial: use a local isolated copy, with an authorized coordinator reviewing every candidate. Compare output with actual final orders; no production release or external sending originates from the trial. Include at least one late change, question about quantity, old quotation, missing unit and PDF revision. Capture field-level errors and evidence citations, timing, and reviewer edits.

Success hypotheses: zero silently preferred wrong revisions and zero omitted production-critical fields in released shadow orders; every exported field has evidence or manual rationale; at least 90% of clarification questions are judged specific and actionable by two reviewers; median active coordinator time falls at least 20% without worsening errors. Small-sample results are directional, not proof of demand.

Stop criteria: any unexplained overwrite of an approved snapshot, unauthorized release, data exposure, untraceable output, or systematic missed critical field pauses the pilot. Inspect root cause and retest affected cases before resuming. Do not use this demo with private files until identity, isolation and retention controls are implemented.

Next scope: if review accuracy succeeds but parsing dominates effort, implement a schema-constrained extraction adapter and representative document parser tests before connectors. If review authority is ambiguous, prioritize approval policy and audit UX. If time savings or willingness to pay is absent, narrow to one product type or stop. Buyer, price, demand and savings remain hypotheses.
